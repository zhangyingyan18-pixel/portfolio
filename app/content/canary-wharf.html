01. Business Challenge

The brand could not rely only on its own channels for this flagship launch. We needed to address four core questions:

  • How do we make a new store relevant to local people?
  • How do we reach office workers, residents and visitors differently?
  • How do we use CW Group’s ecosystem effectively?
  • How do we turn opening attention into repeat activity?

02. Strategic Approach

The opening was designed as a multi-week local GTM and partnership programme rather than a single launch moment, following a clear connectivity architecture.

03. Audience Strategy

Different audiences needed different entry points to understand why the store was relevant to their everyday lives.

work

Office Workers

Lunch / After-work / Convenience

Reached via corporate tenant communication, lunch options, and quick grocery shopping routes.

home

Residents

Everyday shopping / Community

Connected through residential apps, family food ranges, and community loyalty benefits.

explore

Visitors

Discovery / Experience

Engaged with tasting events, food discovery, and social sharing opportunities in the mall.

school

Students

Affordability / Social relevance

Targeted with student discounts, affordable snacks, giveaways, and creator content.

06. Yingyan's Contribution

I supported the Canary Wharf flagship opening by mapping CW Group’s local media and partnership ecosystem, coordinating content and activation requirements, and connecting the store team, venue stakeholders, brand partners, creators, PR and customer channels around one local growth story.

Partnership Coordination

Followed up with CW Group on media channels, influencer lists, mall advertising, and digital placements across their ecosystem.

Channel Planning

Mapped CWG and owned channels, clarified roles for social platforms, and connected different audience segments with appropriate touchpoints.

Content Planning

Planned content themes from store announcements and previews to office lunch use cases, tastings, and loyalty benefits.

Activation

Coordinated food tastings, Lion Dance events, giveaways, health & safety requirements, and stakeholder alignment.
