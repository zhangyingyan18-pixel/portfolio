01. Business Challenge

Translating complex technical projects for international stakeholders.

The core challenge lay in facilitating meaningful connections between disparate tech ecosystems. Startups, investors, and institutional delegations often speak different "languages" regarding innovation, scale, and market entry. The task required a structured approach to not only identify key players but to communicate complex value propositions clearly across cultural and linguistic boundaries to drive engagement in global events like the Sino-UK Tech Summit.

"Success depended on curating the right mix of early-stage tech ventures and institutional capital, requiring highly targeted, bilingual communication strategies rather than broad-brush marketing."
